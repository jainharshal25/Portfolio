# Personalize this portfolio

Fill these files with your details, then send the completed values back when you want the website copy and visual direction tailored.

## Start here

1. `lib/site.ts`
   - Name, role, domain, email, tagline, short description, location, and social URLs.
   - Hero copy, section headings, and contact copy are in `pageCopy`.
2. `data/profile.ts`
   - Proof numbers, work history, education, certifications, and languages.
3. `data/projects.ts`
   - Project names, status, description, facts, role, live URL, and cover image path.
4. `data/capabilities.ts`
   - Services, skills, specialties, and the project or client that proves each one.
5. `components/LogoMarquee.astro`
   - Replace the tool and platform rows with the technologies, clients, or brands you want to show.

## Replace the assets

- `public/assets/brand/avatar-96.png` and `avatar-192.png`: profile image or avatar.
- `public/assets/brand/favicon*`: browser and device icons.
- `public/assets/brand/og-image.png`: social sharing image.
- `public/projects/`: project covers. Keep the paths used in `data/projects.ts` or change them there.
- `public/media/film/`: background film. The site also works with the included placeholder film.

## What to send for a tailored version

Share your preferred visual mood, name and role, short introduction, location, contact email, social links, 3-5 projects, experience, skills, education, and the assets you want used. Mention anything to remove, such as the background film, logo marquee, or a section you do not need.
